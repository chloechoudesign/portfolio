JibJab App Welcome Flow
JibJab’s mobile app welcome experience introduces new users to the platform through a playful feature carousel that highlights its core products — personalized videos, photo-based content, sharing, and reminders.
As Senior Product Designer, I defined the flow, designed the screens, and created the visual assets for the experience. I partnered closely with animation to translate the concept into a polished first-time app experience.
